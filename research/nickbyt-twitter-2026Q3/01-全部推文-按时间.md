10 YouTube habits to start in 2027:

AI answers any question in seconds, so viewers subscribe for your take on it.

5. Write titles in first person

“I tested” and “I lost” went from below average in 2020 to 20% above in 2026

We’re in the authenticity cycle, viewers want to see a real person talking to them.

7. Make videos for the TV

YouTube raised the thumbnail upload limit from 2MB to 50MB for 4K TV screens, so check your thumbnail and text read from across a room.

8. Use one end screen

Send viewers to your most relevant video, or the one with the highest retention.

9. Post image polls on your community tab

You should do 5 checks before picking your YouTube niche:

Count active channels

Channels that haven't uploaded in 6 months aren't competing for today's viewers.

Check who’s active and if they’re performing well.

If they are, great there’s demand.

Check what small channels get

Look at channels with 5,000 to 50,000 subs.

If their typical upload clears about 6,800 views, there's real demand for someone your size.

Compare views to subscribers

A typical small channel gets about half its subscriber count in views per video.

If uploads in a niche beat the subscriber count, it's pulling in new viewers.

Be honest about the workload

Quiet niches with big reach usually need slow, expensive videos.

Make sure you can keep up with the niche's pace for a year.

Go one level down

Pick a sub-niche, then study the small channels beating their size before you film anything.
